Data refresh

  • The data is refreshed once a day in the morning (Bangkok time) straight from HubSpot. The date at the top of the dashboard shows when.
  • Scope: the SEA Sales Pipeline, deal Region Thailand & Others, Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia and Singapore. All amounts are USD (HubSpot "Amount in company currency").
  • On-Hold deals are left out of the pipeline numbers and the PPT.
  • A change made in HubSpot today shows on the portal after the next morning's refresh.

Targets

  • SEA FY target: USD 3,000,000, spread evenly by month (250,000 a month).
  • Country targets: split equally across Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines and Indonesia — USD 750,000 each (62,500 a month).
  • Fiscal year runs April to March; quarters are fiscal (Q1 = Apr–Jun).

Meetings

Which meetings are included

  • Meetings linked to an open SEA deal.
  • Meetings logged only against a company or contact in Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines, Indonesia or Singapore. When one open deal fits (a company on the deal, a company named like the deal, or a contact on the deal), the meeting is shown on that deal.
  • Completed meetings: last 30 days only. Upcoming: everything scheduled.
  • Meetings owned by Abdul Khan and Akash Kumar are not included.

Keeping HubSpot clean

The report can only be as good as HubSpot. These make the biggest difference:

  1. Link every sales meeting to its deal, not just the company or contact.
  2. Set the deal Region to the SEA country (a deal under another region, e.g. India-North, is left out).
  3. Set the contact's Country (e.g. Philippines) so meetings with that person are picked up.
  4. Fill Event Name on contacts met at events, and Deal Source / Sub-source on deals.
  5. Keep Next step, Close date, Implementation fee and Annual subscription fee current on open deals.
  6. Merge duplicate contacts and companies (e.g. two records for the same person).
